> *🔄 The Classic Swap Trick*

Ever have 2 glasses with different drinks? 🥤🥤

You want to exchange them, but you can't pour directly!

So what do you do? You take a 3rd empty glass! ✨

*How it works:*

1.  🥤 Glass A has Mango Juice, Glass B has Water
2.  🍷 Take an Empty Glass (Temp Glass)
3.  ➡️ Pour Mango Juice into the Empty Glass
4.  ➡️ Pour Water into Glass A
5.  ➡️ Pour Mango Juice from Temp Glass into Glass B

Done! 🎉 Drinks are swapped! 
This is the oldest and most logical way to swap. It works in every language! 🌍
